◆ encoder_driver_get_angles()

void encoder_driver_get_angles ( float angles[2])

Get the angles from both encoders.

Note
The returned angles are the input shaft angles in radians. The function does not account for the transmission ratio of the motors.
Parameters
anglesPointer to an array of two floats where the shaft angles will be stored in radians. angles[0] will hold the angle of ENCODER_A and angles[1] will hold the angle of ENCODER_B.
